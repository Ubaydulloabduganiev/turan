TURKISTAN

Silk Road Khanates

Raise a city on the steppe, send caravans to the Grand Bazaar, and break the rival khan.

Difficulty
v0.1.0

How to play

Goal

Destroy the rival khan's Citadel. If all of your Citadels fall, you lose.

Economy

Dehqans (workers) gather food, wood, stone and gold and carry them to a Citadel or Storehouse. Build Farms for endless food and Yurts to house more people. A Caravanserai sends caravans to the Grand Bazaar in the middle of the map for gold that never runs out.

Army

Barracks train Spearmen and Archers. Stables train Horse Archers and Batyrs. Spearmen beat cavalry, arrows are weak against walls, and Batyrs smash buildings.
